Most people who are curious about functional medicine ask themselves the exact same question before they ever pick up the phone: What am I actually walking into?

It’s a fair thing to wonder. Nothing about this looks like the fifteen-minute appointments you’ve experienced, and the last thing you want is to spend money on care that doesn’t help.

So let’s walk through it together, step by step, exactly as it happens at PrimeHealth Denver, from your very first call to the moment you leave with a plan built around you.

“Functional medicine allows us to look beyond a diagnosis and understand why symptoms are happening in the first place. I believe patients deserve to understand all of their options, from lifestyle and nutrition to supplements, medications, and specialty care, so they can partner with us to create a treatment plan that aligns with their goals and values.”

– PrimeHealth Denver Physician

Step 1: The Free 20-Minute Consultation

Before you spend a dollar or commit to anything, you get a free 20-minute call with a team member.

This isn’t a sales pitch dressed up as a conversation. You tell us what’s been going on, your symptoms, your history, everything you’ve already tried that didn’t work. We ask questions and help you honestly determine whether the PrimeHealth approach is likely to help in your specific situation.

And if it’s not the right fit? We’ll tell you that too. That kind of honesty is part of how functional medicine works differently, starting from the very first call.

Step 2: Your First Full Appointment (90 Minutes)

If you decide to move forward, your first visit is 90 minutes long. That’s not a typo, and it’s not an industry standard; it’s simply how we’ve chosen to do things at PrimeHealth because 15 minutes has never been enough time to find a root cause.

This is where the real work begins. Your practitioner will dig into your full health history: childhood, past diagnoses, medications, the major life events that shaped where you are now. They’ll ask about your diet, your sleep, your stress, your daily routine. They’ll want to understand your family history and any genetic factors that might matter. And they’ll walk through your specific symptoms in real detail, when they started, what makes them better, what makes them worse.

That depth of intake is what makes the rest of the process work. You simply can’t find a root cause without seeing the full picture first, and a 15-minute appointment was never built to show you that picture. A 90-minute one is.

Step 3: Advanced Testing

After your first visit, you’ll complete a round of comprehensive testing. What gets ordered depends entirely on your situation, but it always goes well beyond a standard lab panel.

Here’s what that tends to look like at PrimeHealth.

Take your thyroid. A standard panel checks TSH and calls it done. Ours checks TSH too, but also Free T3, Free T4, Total T3, Total T4, Reverse T3, and both major thyroid antibodies, because antibodies can be quietly signaling autoimmune activity years before your numbers ever look “abnormal.”

Or take your energy. If you’re tired all the time, your weight won’t budge, or your mood has shifted in ways that don’t make sense, a basic checkup can’t tell you why, but a comprehensive hormone panel can, because it looks at thyroid function, cortisol, and the hormones tied to sleep and metabolism instead of just the handful of markers a standard blood test covers.

Bloating and irregular digestion get written off as IBS more often than they should. Gut microbiome and digestive function testing exist so that it doesn’t happen to you; they look for the actual cause instead of settling for a simple IBS label.

Low iron, low B12, low vitamin D: any one of these can mimic a completely different condition. Nutrient and inflammatory marker panels catch deficiencies early, before they become bigger problems.

And then there’s stress, the kind that doesn’t ease up no matter how much you sleep. Adrenal function testing measures, directly, how that stress has been reshaping the way your body manages energy.

Put those five together, and you get a picture a standard panel could easily miss completely, while still coming back “normal.”

Step 4: Your Customized Plan

Once your results come in, your practitioner builds your plan. This is where everything you’ve shared and everything we’ve found finally comes together into something you can actually act on.

Depending on what we uncover, your plan might include targeted nutrition changes, specific supplements matched to your deficiencies, lifestyle adjustments, and, where it’s genuinely needed, medical intervention. Every piece of it traces back to your actual results, not a generic protocol pulled off a shelf.

You’ll walk through the whole plan together, and your practitioner will explain the reasoning behind each part. That’s not an accident. The more you understand what’s actually driving your symptoms, the more likely you are to follow through, and the better your results tend to be.
