Luxury wellness destinations are turning to ancient wisdom, sacred rituals, and shamanic practices to offer travellers something beyond relaxation.

  • Writer Sallie Lewis

In today’s ever-evolving luxury hospitality landscape, there is no shortage of indulgent amenities, from private butlers and all-inclusive minibars to pillow menus tailored to guests’ individual sleep styles. Indulgences aside, one could argue that true luxury isn’t measured in the vacation days themselves but by how those days make you feel long after the trip is over. Across the globe, hospitality destinations are embracing this ethos through immersive wellness experiences rooted in shamanic wisdom, energy work, and ancient healing practices—all designed to foster a deeper sense of self-connection and wholeness. From Mexico and Iceland to Bali and Peru, these eight properties focus on leaving guests feeling physically at peace, mentally clear, and spiritually balanced for the journey ahead.

 

Hotel Esencia, Mexico

___

Mayan Magic at Hotel Esencia, Mexico

On a majestic estate in the heart of Mexico’s Riviera Maya, Esencia Spa beckons beneath its soaring thatched roof. Among its more distinctive offerings are the Ancestral Experiences, rooted in sacred Mayan wisdom and traditions. Foremost among them is the Ancient Purifying Bath, a ceremony that begins with a cleansing session in the spa’s Temazcal sweat lodge, followed by a mineral-rich mud body mask, cooling herbal infusion rinse, and full body massage. Other ancestral rituals include a limpia, or energetic cleanse using herbal bouquets and poultices.

___

 

 

___

Energy Unleashed at Golden Door, San Marcos, California

As one of the world’s most exclusive and iconic health resorts, Golden Door is renowned for its offerings designed to restore balance, deepen self-awareness, and support healing on physical, emotional, energetic, and spiritual levels. Here, guests can expect everything from kundalini activations and aura and tea-leaf readings to crystal singing bowls and chakra sessions. The last of these draws on traditional Indian gem mala beads to offer insight into energetic imbalances and pathways for personal growth.

___

 

Hotel Esencia, Mexico

 

___

Shamanism in Manhattan at Aman New York

Much like its flagship locations in Thailand, the Aman Spa New York is a temple of wellness—one that feels worlds away from America’s largest city. At the New York outpost, guests have access to a roster of deeply intuitive practitioners, including Niko Karelas of The Shaman NYC, who offers specialized energy healing and somatic therapies. Even in the “city that never sleeps,” you’ll be hard-pressed to leave feeling anything but rested, rejuvenated, and reconnected to your authentic self.

___

 

Post Ranch Inn, Big Sur, California. Photo by Kodiak Greenwood.

 

___

The Spirit of Big Sur at Post Ranch Inn, Big Sur, California

Perched above one of the planet’s most iconic coastal routes, Post Ranch Inn is superlative in every way, drawing discerning travellers with its natural beauty and restorative energy. Among the property’s many assets is its resident shaman, Rob Somers, who works at the intersection of the spiritual and physical worlds, helping guests release stress, transcend trauma, and return to a healed state. Somers, who studied directly with Indigenous shamans in the Peruvian Andes and Amazon, also draws on the potent energy of the surrounding Big Sur landscape during his sessions.

___

 

Post Ranch Inn, Big Sur, California. Photo by Kodiak Greenwood.

 

___

Sacred Bali at the Bvlgari Resort

In Indonesia’s Island of the Gods, the Bvlgari Resort Bali beckons with blooming frangipani trees and jaw-dropping views of the Indian Ocean. In addition to its extensive spa offerings and cultural experiences, guests at this ultra-luxe, exclusive destination won’t want to miss the Melukat Purifying Ceremony. Held inside the resort’s on-site temple, the ritual is led by a Balinese priest and is believed to cleanse the body and soul, banish negative spirits, and invite abundance and good fortune.

___

 

 

___

Beyond the Physical at Canyon Ranch

Since 1979, Canyon Ranch has been a trailblazer in the wellness arena. Today, the brand offers an array of metaphysical and energy-healing experiences at its Tucson and Lenox locations, from astrocartography and clairvoyant readings to intuitive reiki, and biofield tuning―all designed to restore and replenish the mind, body, and spirit. Canyon Ranch is also renowned for its highly sought-after retreats, led by wellness experts as well as celebrity guests including Sutton Foster, whose personal shaman and spiritual mentor, Desi Valentine, has made appearances at her events.

___

 

Eleven Deplar Farm, Fljót Valley, Iceland.

 

___

Viking Wellness at Eleven Deplar Farm, Fljót Valley, Iceland

Situated on Iceland’s remote Troll Peninsula, this 15th-century sheep farm turned adventure lodge draws inspiration from Viking and Nordic rituals. The 5,000-square-foot spa features world-class amenities, including i-sopod flotation tanks, as well as a roster of restorative services ranging from sound healing sessions to yoga nidra. The signature Viking Sauna Ritual is particularly memorable, beginning with guided meditation in the outdoor Viking Sauna, followed by alternating rounds of intense heat and glacial-water plunges.

___

 

Eleven Deplar Farm, Fljót Valley, Iceland.

.

 

___

A Gift to the Earth at Belmond Sanctuary Lodge, Machu Picchu, Peru

The Belmond Sanctuary Lodge appears like a dream at the misty gates of Machu Picchu.  Drawing on the ancient powers of this storied citadel, the lodge offers a range of spiritual services led by Andean shamans. In local culture, the coca leaf is sacred and believed to carry messages from Mother Earth and the mountain spirits. The property’s special coca leaf readings aim to answer guests’ questions about life, love, health, future, and work. Another coveted ceremony, aptly titled Payment to the Earth, invites guests to set personal intentions and prepare offerings of flowers, seeds, grains, and fruit while the shaman recites ancient prayers and blessings.
