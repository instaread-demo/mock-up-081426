I’ve been air frying since 2017 and developing recipes for more than a decade, so trust me when I tell you the air fryer can do a whole lot more than crisp up chicken wings. This air fryer mac and cheese gives you creamy, gooey mac and cheese with a golden top and plenty of melted cheese throughout, all without heating up the whole kitchen. I’ve tested the method so you don’t end up with dry noodles or a broken, greasy cheese sauce.

Air Fryer mac and Cheese Ingredients

  • Elbow Macaroni or Cellentani Pasta
  • Butter
  • Flour
  • Milk
  • Heavy Whipping Cream
  • Onion Powder
  • Salt and Pepper
  • Cheddar Cheese: Use any of your favorite combinations of cheese. My favorite hack for all of the cheese is to use this food processor to grate it all up. Done in no tine!
  • Parmesan Cheese
  • Gruyere: I love using smoked gruyere!

Equipment Needed

You will need an oven-safe pan or dish that will fit your air fryer. I like to use these ramekins, but anything oven-safe will work.

How to Make Air Fryer Mac and Cheese

Detailed measurements and full instructions can be found in the recipe card at the bottom of this post.

  1. Grate all of the cheese: I always recommend this because pre-shredded cheese does not melt the same.
  2. Cook the pasta: Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the elbow macaroni or cellentani pasta until just al dente. Drain and set aside.
  3. Make a roux: In a large saucepan, melt butter over medium heat. Whisk in the flour and cook for 1–2 minutes until it’s golden and smells slightly nutty. This step thickens the cheese sauce.
  4. Build the sauce: Slowly whisk in the milk and heavy cream until smooth. Keep whisking to avoid lumps. Let it simmer for 3–4 minutes until slightly thickened. Stir in the onion powder, salt, and pepper to season.
  5. Melt in the cheeses: Reduce the heat to low. Add the half of the cheddar, parmesan, and gruyere, stirring until melted and creamy. Save a little of each cheese for topping later.
  6. Combine pasta and sauce: Add the cooked pasta to the cheese sauce and stir until every piece is coated in that creamy mixture.
  7. Layer the mac and cheese: You can use any oven-safe dish or pan that will fit in your air fryer. Pour half of the pasta mixture into the pan or dish. Sprinkle with a layer of the remaining cheddar, gruyere, and parmesan cheese. Add the rest of the pasta and finish with more cheese on top.
  8. Air Fry: Place the dish in the air fryer and air fry for 15-20 minutes until the cheese is gooey and bubbling. I like to air fry until the top is crisp as well.
  9. Rest and serve. Let it sit for 5–10 minutes before serving so the cheese sets up just a little. Then dig in to the gooey, cheesy goodness.

Equipment

  • Cosori 7quart air fryer
  • Ramekins

Ingredients
  

  • 1 pound elbow macaroni or cellentani pasta
  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • 3 tablespoons flour
  • 2-3 cups milk I often use 2 cups. Use your judgement. Start with 2 and add more if needed.
  • 1 cup heavy whipping cream
  • salt and pepper to taste
  • onion powder to taste Start with ½ teaspoon. Taste and adjust.
  • 2 cups grated sharp cheddar cheese You can you whatever type of cheeses you like. Mix and match your favorite types.
  • 1 cup grated parmigiano reggiano
  • 2-3 cups grated gruyere cheese I like smoked gruyere.

Instructions
 

  • I always recommend buying block cheese and grating the cheese first. this because pre-shredded cheese does not melt the same.
  • Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the elbow macaroni or cellentani pasta until just al dente. Drain and set aside.
  • In a large saucepan or pot (I use the same one I used to cook the pasta), melt butter over medium heat. Whisk in the flour and cook for 1–2 minutes until it’s golden and smells slightly nutty. This step thickens the cheese sauce.
  • Slowly whisk in the milk and heavy cream until smooth. Keep whisking to avoid lumps. Let it simmer for 3–4 minutes until slightly thickened. Stir in the onion powder, salt, and pepper (to taste) to season.
  • Reduce the heat to low. Add the half of the cheddar, parmesan, and gruyere, stirring until melted and creamy. Save a little of each cheese for topping later. Taste repeatedly. Make sure the sauce isn't bland and adjust it to suit your taste.
  • Add the cooked pasta to the cheese sauce and stir until every piece is coated in that creamy mixture.
  • You can use any oven-safe dish or pan that will fit in your air fryer. Pour half of the pasta mixture into the pan or dish. Sprinkle with a layer of the remaining cheddar, gruyere, and parmesan cheese. Add the rest of the pasta and finish with more cheese on top.
  • Place the dish in the air fryer and air fry at 350 degrees for 15-20 minutes or until the cheese is gooey and bubbling. I like to air fry until the top is crisp as well.
  • Let it sit for 5–10 minutes before serving so the cheese sets up just a little. Then dig in to the gooey, cheesy goodness.

Frequently Asked Questions and Recipe Pro Tips

Can you really make mac and cheese in the air fryer?

Absolutely. The air fryer is basically a small convection oven, so it’s great for getting creamy mac and cheese with that bubbly, golden cheese on top.

What type of dish can I use in the air fryer?

Use an oven-safe baking dish that fits comfortably inside your air fryer basket. Ceramic, glass, metal, and disposable aluminum pans can work as long as they’re rated for the cooking temperature you’re using.

What cheese is best for air fryer mac and cheese?

Cheddar is always a solid choice, but I love combining cheeses for better flavor and texture. Sharp cheddar, Monterey Jack, Colby Jack, mozzarella, Gouda, and Gruyère are all options. Use cheeses that melt well and bring some flavor to the party.

Can I use pre-shredded cheese?

You can, but freshly grated cheese usually melts much better. Bagged shredded cheese contains anti-caking ingredients that can make the sauce less smooth. If you want that creamy, melty pull, grab the block and shred it yourself.

Why is my air fryer mac and cheese dry?

It was likely cooked too long, at too high a temperature, or didn’t have enough liquid. Mac and cheese will continue to thicken as it sits, so pull it while it’s still nice and creamy.

How do I keep the cheese sauce from getting grainy?

Avoid overheating the cheese and use freshly shredded cheese when possible. High heat can cause the cheese to separate and turn oily or grainy. We want creamy mac, not cheese clumps floating in grease.

How do I get a crispy, golden top?

Sprinkle extra shredded cheese over the top during the final few minutes of cooking.

Can I make air fryer mac and cheese ahead of time?

Yes. Assemble it ahead of time, cover, and refrigerate until you’re ready to cook. Since it will be cold, you may need to add a few extra minutes to the cooking time.

How do I store leftover air fryer mac and cheese?

Let it cool, then store it tightly covered in the refrigerator for 3–4 days.

What’s the best way to reheat it?

Add a splash of milk or cream before reheating to loosen the sauce. Reheat it in an air fryer-safe dish until warm, but don’t leave it in there forever. That extra liquid is what helps bring the creamy texture back.

Can I freeze air fryer mac and cheese?

Yes. Let it cool completely and freeze it tightly covered. Thaw it in the refrigerator before reheating, and add a little milk or cream because the sauce will usually thicken after freezing.

Pair With These Recipes

I love this mac with baked turkey wings, crispy fried chicken, Southern pork chops, collard greens with smoked turkey, Southern candied sweet potatoes, and cooked cabbage.
