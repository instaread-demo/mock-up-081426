Family Events & Activities Near You

Summer Stages

When: August 2026
Where: Confederation Park, Burnaby

Celebrate summer at Confederation Park with a free outdoor performance series for the whole family from July 14 to August 27. Bring a blanket or lawn chair and enjoy Summer Stages' live entertainment every Tuesday and Wednesday throughout the day, plus Thursday Family Nights from 5:30pm. Along with a diverse lineup of music, puppetry, theatre, magic, dance, circus acts, storytelling, and cultural performances, families can enjoy free art activities and lawn games between shows. Highlights include puppet theatre, ventriloquists, Elvis tributes, opera, clown circus, children’s concerts, cultural music and dance, and lively evening performances. Events take place rain or shine (weather permitting) at Confederation Park, Burnaby, beside the spray park.

Cineplex Family Favourites

When: Saturdays in August
Where: Various Cineplex Theaters

Weekend plans for the whole gang just got easier! Our Family Favourites program runs movies Saturdays, for only $3.99 plus applicable taxes (in-theatre price). Family Favourites programming is available on select Saturdays at participating locations. Films subject to change.  If purchased online, tickets are $3.99 for CineClub members (booking fee waived), $4.99 for Scene+ members ($3.99 + $1.00 online booking fee), or $5.49 for all other online purchases ($3.99 + $1.50 online booking fee). Online booking fee allows for advanced seat selection and sharable digital tickets, and applies to the first 4 tickets purchased in a transaction.  All prices are + tax.
