AOC Air Mega AR Glasses at $159: 201-Inch Screen and 120Hz Gaming in Your Pocket

Reading Time: 5 minutesAOC Air Mega AR Glasses offer a huge 201-inch virtual screen and 120Hz gaming for $159. Find out what they do well and where they fall short.

AR glasses have been regarded as a costly gadget for individuals who wish to experiment with technology before it becomes accessible to the common man. The AOC Air Mega is a less ambitious approach. The $159 price targets those who primarily wish to have a larger screen to take with them. The aviator style frame is not as strange as some AR frames and is relatively light. Its main attraction is the display.

The glasses can display a virtual screen up to 201 inches in size and achieve Full HD 1080p resolution at 120 frames per second. This is sufficient to make films look great and games run smoothly. However, the device is not meant to offer the same AR experience as the more expensive devices. It doesn’t offer head-tracking and lacks interpupillary distance (IPD) adjustment, making it less ideal for those who require more control or wish to utilize it for more challenging tasks.

AOC Air Mega AR Glasses Focus on Affordable Big-Screen Gaming

When the glasses are viewed as a common product, rather than a set of specifications, that distinction becomes more apparent. They are most effective when their role is just to present a big, responsive screen in front of you. The high refresh rate and responsive display are their forte, as are movies and other entertainment. Casual gaming is another area where they are well suited. They are lightweight which is good for them to carry but it is not everything.

The field of view is rather small, and the glasses don’t really look like a viable alternative to a full-fledged productivity setup. This is where their low cost is their greatest asset and a hint as to what they are missing. If you’re looking for a budget-friendly option to enjoy AR-style viewing, the AOC Air Mega makes good sense. If you’re hoping for the best of the AR experience, know that $159 gets you a useful display, but not a complete AR system.

Portable Design Makes the AOC Air Mega Easy to Carry

The design of The AOC Air Mega definitely includes portability. The glasses include a folding case and a robust USB-C cable, making them easy to store and transport. The compact form comes with a hefty price tag, though. The birdbath optics make the frame somewhat thicker than a normal pair of glasses. This isn’t particularly uncommon with lower-priced AR products. The AOC Air Mega is designed to be functional, not fancy.

201-Inch Virtual Display and 120Hz Refresh Rate

The primary benefit of the AOC Air Mega is that it provides you with a big screen without the big device. It can create a virtual display of up to 201 inches and has a 46° field of view. The 1080p resolution maintains a decent level of detail in the image, and the 120 frames per second refresh rate provides smooth motion. Colours are also vibrant enough to make movies and games interesting. However, the glasses don’t offer some of the capabilities of more advanced AR devices. 

The interpupillary distance is fixed and there is no head-tracking (3 DOF or 6 DOF). This is important because an AR display is more useful if it can respond to the position and movement of your head. If it didn’t have that capability, the AOC Air Mega would be more of a portable screen than a fully interactive AR device. This may not be a major concern if you are just looking for a simple pastime. It is a definite drawback for work or applications that require precise spatial awareness.

AOC Air Mega Gaming Performance and Everyday Use

120Hz Makes Casual Gaming Smoother

The glasses are fairly good at gaming. The 120 Hz refresh rate creates a smooth feel to fast movement. Tough, one issue is the 46° field of view. It provides a big picture but not a broad one. Players who are accustomed to higher-end AR equipment might not be as engaged as they would like to be.

Glasses Fall Short for Productivity

The glasses cannot keep virtual screens fixed in a particular place, and they have no head-tracking system. This can make it challenging to create a productive workspace with multiple windows.

A Good Screen for Films

The AOC Air Mega really impresses when using it normally for media use. It’s bright to display and the built-in speakers are decent for casual viewing. The large virtual image is ideal for films and streamed video. No need to master an elaborate system or tweak numerous settings. Just wear the glasses and enjoy. 

AOC Air Mega vs Xreal Glasses: What You Give Up

It is easier to understand the AOC Air Mega when compared to the higher-priced Xreal glasses. It doesn’t just lose on all counts. The picture is good and the sound is decent. At $159, that’s a nice benefit. With cheaper hardware, however, there has to be something missing, and here it is. 

The glasses have a narrower field of view and are not able to handle 6 DOF motion. They also don’t have IPD adjustment. They’re not just the icing on the cake for those who wish to use AR more seriously. They impact the device’s capabilities. The AOC Air Mega is therefore a basic product. It can provide some basic entertainment, but not the flexibility of a high-end AR system.

Is the AOC Air Mega Worth $159?

The AOC Air Mega is an affordable introduction to this type of technology at just $159. That matters. A person who is primarily interested in watching movies, streaming video or playing games can get a lot of the essential experience without spending as much money as you would on the expensive hardware.

The glasses are less advanced. They should be thought of more as an introduction and not a catch all device. They might be sufficient if you just want a big private screen. If you’re looking for more advanced tracking, more immersion and a proper virtual workspace, you’ll have to pay more.

Important Limitations of AOC Air Mega AR Glasses

The AOC Air Mega has a low price, but with some definite compromises. The IPD is not adjustable and the glasses are not head tracking. They also don’t work well for serious productivity, where these missing features are more important. Some believe that the glasses are worth the $159, while others find them less than satisfactory. Finally, they’re more useful as an inexpensive introduction to AR than as a tool for heavy users.

Final Words

The AOC Air Mega AR Glasses present a pretty solid argument for simplicity in AR. They’re not looking to compete with high end AR systems at $159 or to make your living room into a futuristic command centre. Instead, they offer a massive 201-inch virtual display, 1080p resolution and a high 120Hz refresh rate in a lightweight, portable package. 

That’s why they are especially attractive for movies, streaming and casual gaming, where the impressive display takes the bulk of the load. But the limited 46° FOV and the absence of head tracking soon remind you that this isn’t a full-fledged AR workstation. 

The AOC Air Mega delivers the right stuff without breaking the bank. So, it’s probably worth a look if you’re looking for cheap AR entertainment. But don’t count on it supplanting a high-end AR system in the near future.
