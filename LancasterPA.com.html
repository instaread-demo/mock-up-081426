7 Outdoor Things to Do in Lancaster, PA with Kids

Written by Jen Lewis July 24, 2026
Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 3:08
Instaread

If you’re looking for some outdoor things to do in Lancaster, PA,  Pennsylvania Dutch Country has more than enough to keep you and your family busy.

Here in Lancaster County, you’re sure to find rich history, unique Amish culture, beautiful outdoor sights, and more. Check out this list of our favorite outdoor things to do in Lancaster, Pa., and start planning an exciting family adventure today.

Outdoor Adventures

1. Work those pedals

Hit the trails, specifically the Warwick to Ephrata Rail Trail. Pedal at your own pace through the farmland and see the towns of Ephrata and Lititz, all from the scenic rail trail. If you dare to wander off the path, you’ll find historic architecture, breweries, and a few places to spot for sweet treats.

2. Scoot around

A scooter ride is a great way to discover Lancaster County, nicknamed “Garden Spot of America.” Enjoy a backroad guide with Strasburg Scooters discovering hidden gems, such as the area’s covered bridges or schoolhouse, which you may miss if you travel by car. Behind the wheel of a single-seat, double-seat, or scoot coupe, the kids won’t be able to stop talking about their ride around Lancaster County.

 

Amish Country Tours

3. Ride the hills

Take it slow with AAA Buggy Rides and travel down the country roads in an Amish buggy. Soak in the scenery and learn about Lancaster County’s Amish history from your friendly and knowledgeable drivers.

Your guided tour will lead you to meet actual Amish business people. It may take you to various stops, such as an Amish dairy farm, a woodworking shop, a quilt store, and more.

4. Visit the farm

Immerse yourself in the Amish way of life at Amish Farm and House. Visitors can tour a genuine Amish house and 15-acre, 300-year-old farm at the first educational farm museum dedicated to Amish culture. Here you’ll find an authentic and accurate experience while remaining respectful and true to the historical and modern customs of Lancaster County’s Amish.

Outdoor Experiences

5. Enjoy the ride

Ready for a day out at an amusement park? Dutch Wonderland is a 48-acre “Kingdom for Kids” with over 35 rides and activities, a water play area, and live entertainment. At an amusement park designed for families with young children, it’s easy to find rides that fit your child or even ones the whole family can enjoy.

6. Fun on the farm

Cherry Crest Adventure Farm is where fun, family, and farming all come together. Kids and parents alike have their pick of over 50 outdoor activities, including an iconic 5-acre corn maze, pig races, a butter-making show, apple blasters, farm tour wagons, and more.

As if that wasn’t enough, Cherry Crest Adventure Farm now hosts various festivals, live music, delicious food, and fireworks during the summer and fall seasons.

Are you ready for your adventure to Lancaster County, PA?

Your trip to explore all the outdoor activities in Lancaster County won’t plan itself. There’s so much to learn about and experience here. If you need more ideas, you can always find more outdoor things to do in Lancaster, Pa.
