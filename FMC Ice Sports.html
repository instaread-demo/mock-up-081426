$10,000 DONATED!

July 20, 2026 | by: FMC Marketing
Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 1:05
Instaread

We are honored to have partnered with the Scott Hamilton CARES Foundation for a second year to make an impact in finding a cure and Turn Cancer Upside Down®. We’re even more honored to have found a way for our skaters to use their skating as a positive force and contribute, and for our local clubs and arenas to come together as one in this common bond to such an amazing cause. Together, with your participation and support the collective team points, volunteering, judging and additional donations will allow us to make a $10,000 donation to the Scott Hamilton CARES Foundation and its local affiliates. 

The event was held at the Aleixo Arena in Raynham, MA Fri 6/26 to Sun 6/28 and hosted 170+ skaters from 9 Local Clubs/Arenas through 343 starts and 150 individual, partner and team events. 

Head to our FMC Competition page, link below, to see donation earnings for each participating team as well as special thank-you messages recognizing those who helped make this event a success.
