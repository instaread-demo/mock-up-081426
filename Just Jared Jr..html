Malachi Barton and Liamani Segura step out for the premiere of their new movie Camp Rock 3!

The two lead the new generation of Camp Rockers in the upcoming third installment of the Disney franchise, as Fletch and Sage, respectively.

Malachi was seen sporting a cropped Camp Rock tee at the event, while Liamani wore a Giorgio Armani gown, as they stepped out for their big day on Tuesday (August 10) at Walt Disney Studios in Burbank, Calif.

Also stepping out were their fellow new generation stars, Hudson Stone, Lumi Pollack, Casey Trotter, Brooklynn Pitts, Ava Jean, Ella Lucas, and Orlando Lucas, as well as Sherry Cola and OG stars Demi Lovato, Jonas Brothers and Maria Canals-Barrera, who was joined by her husband, David Barrera, and their daughters, Bridget and Madeleine.

From Camp Rock 1 and 2, Roshon Fegan, Anna Maria Perez de Tagle, and Matthew Finley also showed their support!

What is Camp Rock 3 about?

When Connect 3 loses their opening act for a major reunion tour, they return to their beloved Camp Rock to discover the next big thing. As campers vie for the chance to open for their favorite band, tensions rise and friendships are tested — leading to unexpected alliances, revelations, and romances.

Get the scoop on all of the new characters!

When does Camp Rock 3 come out?

The upcoming movie will premiere THIS Thursday (August 13) at 8 pm ET/PT on Disney Channel, and it will stream the next day on Disney+. Watch the trailer here!

If you missed it, Liamani, Malachi, Ella, and Orlando are repping Camp Rock on Disney’s Worlds Collide Tour, which kicks off next month. Rehearsals are currently underway.

FYI: Demi is wearing a vintage Dolce&Gabbana dress with YSL shoes. The Jonas Brothers are wearing Giorgio Armani. Liamani is wearing a Giorgio Armani gown. Orlando is wearing Bruno Magli loafers.
