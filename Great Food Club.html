Steam-train dining with Rothley wine on the Great Central Railway

Words and pictures by Tim Burke

Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 1:25
Instaread

Leicestershire has beautiful countryside and some superb food and drink producers. Rather brilliantly, the Great Central Railway offers the chance to enjoy both through a variety of dining events on board its stunning steam trains.

The line passes within 300 metres of Rothley Wine Estate and The Rothley Rosé evening offers a three-course menu matched with five of the vineyard’s award-winning wines.

Winemaker Liz Robson does a fine job of talking guests through flavours ranging from the elderflower nose of the excellent High Hopes fizz to the refreshing, lemony King Richard white and the light-bodied, cherry flavours of the King Henry red.

Standout dishes included a superb duck terrine and a beautifully light chocolate cheesecake, matched with the gently sweet Battle Royal rosé.

From the glorious views over Swithland Reservoir to the plush seating, white-gloved service and soundtrack of clickety-clacks and toots, the experience is a fine showcase for Leicestershire.

The Great Central Railway also runs other dining experiences, including afternoon teas, cheese tastings, Sunday lunches and fish and chip suppers.

Visit gcrailway.co.uk for details.
