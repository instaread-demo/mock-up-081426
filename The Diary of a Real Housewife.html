Why You’ll Love This Recipe

Simple Ingredients, Big Flavor
This casserole comes together with everyday ingredients like chicken, Swiss cheese, stuffing mix, and cream soup. It’s an easy recipe that delivers plenty of comforting flavor without a complicated ingredient list.

Perfect for Busy Weeknights
With only a few minutes of prep, you can have dinner in the oven and let it bake while you take care of everything else. It’s an ideal meal for hectic evenings.

Creamy Comfort Food
The rich cream sauce keeps the chicken tender while the buttery stuffing topping adds the perfect crunchy finish. Every bite is warm, creamy, and satisfying.

Family-Friendly Dinner
Even picky eaters tend to love this casserole because of its familiar flavors and simple ingredients. It’s one of those recipes everyone asks for again.

Great for Leftovers
This casserole reheats beautifully, making it perfect for lunches or another easy dinner later in the week.

Ingredient Notes:

  • Chicken Breast – Boneless, skinless chicken breasts work perfectly in this casserole. If they’re very thick, pound them to an even thickness so they cook evenly.
  • Cream of Chicken Soup – This creates a rich, creamy sauce that keeps the chicken moist while it bakes.
  • Chicken Broth – helps keep the stuffing and chicken moist.
  • Onion Powder
  • Garlic Powder
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Swiss Cheese – Swiss cheese gives this casserole its signature creamy, slightly nutty flavor. If you prefer, provolone or mozzarella can be substituted.
  • Chicken Stuffing Mix – A boxed stuffing mix creates an easy, buttery topping with plenty of flavor and texture.
  • Butter – Mixing melted butter with the stuffing helps it bake into a perfectly crisp, golden topping.

Directions:

Step One – Add chicken to a 9×13 casserole dish.

Step Two – Whisk together cream of chicken soup with broth and all seasonings.

Step Three – Add sliced Swiss cheese on top of chicken then add on the whisked and seasoned chicken soup mixture.

Step Four – Add stuffing on top and then melted butter. Cover and bake at 350 for 50 minutes or until chicken is cooked through.

Variations and Substitutions

  • Use chicken thighs instead of chicken breasts.
  • Substitute cream of mushroom or cream of celery soup for a different flavor.
  • Add sliced mushrooms between the chicken and cheese.
  • Mix shredded Parmesan into the stuffing topping.
  • Add steamed broccoli or green beans to make it a complete one-pan meal.

FAQs

Can I make Swiss Chicken Casserole ahead of time?

Yes! Assemble the casserole up to 24 hours in advance, cover, and refrigerate. Add the stuffing topping just before baking for the best texture.

Can I use chicken thighs?

Absolutely. Boneless, skinless chicken thighs are a great substitute and stay especially juicy.

Can I substitute the Swiss cheese?

Yes! Provolone, mozzarella, Monterey Jack, or white cheddar all work well if your family isn’t a fan of Swiss cheese.

How do I know when the casserole is done?

The chicken should reach an internal temperature of 165°F, and the stuffing should be golden brown and crisp.

Can I freeze this casserole?

Yes! This casserole freezes well after baking. Let it cool completely before freezing in an airtight container.

Swiss Chicken Casserole

By: Jennie Duncan
Swiss Chicken Casserole is an easy, creamy chicken dinner made with tender chicken breasts, Swiss cheese, stuffing, and a rich cream sauce.
Prep: 10 minutes mins
Cook: 50 minutes mins
Servings: 4 servings

Ingredients 

  • 2 pounds chicken breast, (boneless and skinless)
  • 10.5 oz cream of chicken soup, (1 can)
  • ¼ cup chicken broth
  • ½ teaspoon onion powder
  • ¼ teaspoon garlic powder
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • ⅛ teaspoon pepper
  • 6 slices slice cheese
  • 6 oz chicken stuffing mix, (1 box)
  • ¼ cup butter, melted

Instructions 

  • Add chicken to a 9×13 casserole dish.
  • Whisk together cream of chicken soup with broth and all seasonings.
  • Add sliced Swiss cheese on top of chicken then add on the whisked and seasoned chicken soup mixture.
  • Add stuffing on top and then melted butter. Cover and Bake at 350 for for 50 minutes or until chicken is cooked through.

Notes

Storage and Reheating
Refrigerator
Store leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days.
Freezer
Freeze the baked casserole for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator before reheating.
Reheating
Reheat individual servings in the microwave or warm the casserole in a 325°F oven until heated through.
